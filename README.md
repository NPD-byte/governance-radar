# Yunique AI Governance Radar

A unified AI, privacy, children's-online-safety and cybersecurity legal intelligence tracker — 180+ laws, bills and frameworks across the US (federal and state), EU & UK, Africa and Global, plus a 22-case AI litigation docket watch. Every instrument is mapped to the **CLEAR™ Framework** (Clarity · Legal & Risk Alignment · Enablement · Accountability · Responsible Execution).

Built and maintained by [Yunique AI](https://yuniqueai.com) — govern AI with CLEAR™.

## What's inside

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The complete tracker — open it in any browser. Design, filters, effective-date timeline, CLEAR™ exposure briefs, analytics, Review Queue. |
| `data/laws.js` | The laws & bills database (one record per instrument: jurisdiction, stage, dates, enforcement, penalties, obligations, CLEAR™ tags). |
| `data/cases.js` | The litigation docket watch (court, status, next milestone, why it matters). |

## Publish it as a live page (GitHub Pages)

1. In this repository, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under *Build and deployment*, set **Source** to `Deploy from a branch`, choose branch **main** and folder **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
3. After a minute, your tracker is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## Updating

The working copy of this tracker lives in Claude (Cowork), where a scheduled Monday digest refreshes the database, docket watch and intelligence feed each week — with every change logged to the in-app Review Queue for human approval. To update this repository, ask Claude to regenerate the repo files, then replace `index.html` and the `data/` files here and commit.
