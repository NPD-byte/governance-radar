The CLEAR™ Framework

Organisations roll out AI with confidence and zero accountability. CLEAR™ is Yunique AI's framework for closing that gap — governing AI with legally defensible decisions. Every law, bill and case in this radar is mapped to the pillar(s) of work it creates for an organisation. Not what the law says. What it demands of you. Click any pillar's "Filter the database" button to see its instruments; the small plum tags on entries throughout the radar link back to this page.

Method & coverage

The YAI Governance Radar covers laws, bills, regulations, and frameworks across five regions (US Federal, US State, EU & UK, Africa, Global) and five domains (AI & automated decision-making, privacy & data protection, children's online safety, cybersecurity & breach, cross-cutting), plus a curated docket watch of AI cases. Compiled July 2026 from primary sources (congress.gov, state legislatures, EUR-Lex, legislation.gov.uk, national regulators, court dockets) and verified secondary reporting. Each instrument records enforcement authority, maximum penalty, and key obligations — compliance-reference level, not citation-grade. PRA = private right of action. Stages: In effect, Enacted — phasing in, Passed into law, Passed chamber / In committee / Proposed, Enjoined, Failed / Withdrawn. The timeline counts down to each instrument's next major compliance date. Every instrument and case is also mapped to the CLEAR™ Framework — Clarity (transparency, disclosure, labeling), Legal & Risk Alignment (liability, penalties, enforcement), Enablement (literacy, skills, sandboxes), Accountability (audits, assessments, oversight), Responsible Execution (use restrictions, safety guardrails) — so the database reads as the evidence base under a CLEAR™ assessment. Updated weekly by the Monday digest; ask Claude to add, correct, or re-verify any entry.
